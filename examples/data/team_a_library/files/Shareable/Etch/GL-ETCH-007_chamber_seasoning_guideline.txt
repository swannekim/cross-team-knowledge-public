ETCH CHAMBER SEASONING GUIDELINE (GL-ETCH-007, revision 5)
Applies to: ETCH-05, ETCH-06 and ETCH-07, all chambers
Owner: Etch module, Process Engineering

1. Purpose
Seasoning conditions the chamber walls after a wet clean or preventive maintenance so that the first production wafers see a stable plasma and a low particle level.

2. Standard sequence
After any preventive maintenance that opens the chamber, run 25 bare-silicon seasoning wafers with the SEASON_PolyStd recipe (CF4/O2/HBr chemistry, 60 seconds each). After a wet clean only, 15 seasoning wafers are sufficient.

3. Release criteria
Run two particle monitor wafers after seasoning. Release the chamber only if the adder count is below 10 particles of 0.12 um or larger per wafer, and if the etch-rate monitor is within 3% of the chamber baseline.

4. Special cases
When the upper liner or the focus ring is replaced with a part from a new supplier lot, extend seasoning by 10 wafers and repeat the particle check. Record the seasoning count in the tool log.

5. Change history
Revision 5 (2026-09-20): added the liner-replacement rule after excursion YE-0412.
