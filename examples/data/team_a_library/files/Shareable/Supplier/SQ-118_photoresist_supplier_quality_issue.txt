SUPPLIER QUALITY ISSUE SQ-118 - Photoresist lot contamination
Supplier: Northwind Chemicals (fictional), product NR-248 KrF photoresist
Opened: 2026-08-27   Status: corrective action in verification
Report owner: Supplier Quality, Process Engineering

Problem statement
Lot NR248-2608-11 of NR-248 photoresist contained gel particles that caused bridging defects on the NX-7 gate lithography layer. Incoming inspection did not detect the gels because the liquid particle count specification only covers particles of 0.2 um and larger.

Impact
Four NX-7 lots were reworked at the litho step. Defect density at gate litho rose from 0.08 to 0.31 defects per square centimetre for wafers coated from the affected bottles. No lots were scrapped.

Containment
All bottles from lot NR248-2608-11 were quarantined. Coating tools were purged and two filter changes were performed on track TRK-04.

Supplier response (8D summary)
D4 root cause: a filter housing seal on the supplier filling line was replaced with a non-qualified material that shed gel-like fragments.
D5/D6: the seal material was reverted and the supplier added a 0.1 um final filtration step.
D7: the supplier will report liquid particle counts down to 0.1 um on every certificate of analysis.
