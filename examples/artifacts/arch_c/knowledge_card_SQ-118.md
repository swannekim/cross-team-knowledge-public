## Summary

Lot NR248-2608-11 of NR-248 photoresist contained gel particles that caused bridging defects on the NX-7 gate lithography layer. D5/D6: the seal material was reverted and the supplier added a 0.1 um final filtration step. D7: the supplier will report liquid particle counts down to 0.1 um on every certificate of analysis. On-site visit 2026-09-03: visitor registration for the supplier engineer was filed with resident registration number [REDACTED:KR_RRN] (to be purged after 30 days).

## Key facts

- Supplier: Northwind Chemicals (fictional), product NR-248 KrF photoresist
- Lot NR248-2608-11 of NR-248 photoresist contained gel particles that caused bridging defects on the NX-7 gate lithography layer.
- D4 root cause: a filter housing seal on the supplier filling line was replaced with a non-qualified material that shed gel-like fragments.
- D5/D6: the seal material was reverted and the supplier added a 0.1 um final filtration step.
- D7: the supplier will report liquid particle counts down to 0.1 um on every certificate of analysis.
